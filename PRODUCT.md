## Users

One presentation, four audiences in the same room (all confirmed):

- Government leadership: Ministry and National Committee officials deciding on support and scale-up.
- International partners: delegations, donors and agencies looking at Uzbekistan's e-government work.
- Applicants and designers: companies and project developers who submit applications.
- Center staff: inspectors, experts and departments who work inside the system.

The presenter is a member of the State Ecological Expertise Center team, advancing step by step with a keyboard or clicker.

## Product Purpose

A cinematic, step-by-step web presentation of ECO SERVICE (eco-service.uz), the State Ecological Expertise Center's digital platform for state ecological expertise. It follows one application through the eight stages of the process, so every audience understands how an application moves, who acts at each stage, and what the system does on its own. Success: the audience leaves believing, in equal measure, that the process is fully digital, transparent, and fast and predictable.

## Positioning

ECO SERVICE carries the whole state ecological expertise process in one system: submission through my.gov.uz, simultaneous routing to the district ecological inspector and the Application Intake Department, an on-site inspection fixed by coordinates, online payment with an automatic status change, automatic deadlines and a full action history, review by expert supervisors, coordination with two divisions of the National Committee, approval by the Center's director, E-IMZO signing, and delivery back to the applicant through my.gov.uz, all within 15–25 working days.
